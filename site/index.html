No server

Your class is shared directly between your classmates’ computers, encrypted, through Syncthing.

No account

Nothing to sign up for. Each class is an ordinary folder on your computer.

No tracking

Tramado collects nothing about you. AI is optional and uses your own key.

Everything for your classes, in one app

Lectures, documents, notes, exams and your class group live together, so what you need for a test is one search away.

Lectures

Recorded and transcribed on your computer

Record in the room or an online class, or add a recording you already have. Transcription runs on your computer with whisper.cpp: the speech model downloads once, then works offline. In a shared class, one computer transcribes each lecture and everyone gets the transcript.

  • Mark moments with the keys 1–4 and take notes at a timestamp
  • Follow the transcript as the recording plays, and correct it together
  • Export it as Markdown, subtitles or plain text
Documents

Every handout, searchable

Add slides, readings and legislation: PDF, Word (.docx), PowerPoint (.pptx), OpenDocument text, images and more. Ctrl+K finds a phrase across lectures, documents, exams and chat, and opens the PDF on the right page with the passage marked.

  • Laws list their articles
  • Class notes are shared; your own notes stay on your computer
Exams

Practise with answers that cite your sources

Add a past exam paper: a PDF, a Word file, a photo, or typed in. The AI finds the questions, you check them, and then it drafts answers from your own class material, citing the page, the article of law or the minute of the lecture each claim comes from.

  • A “grounded” score for every answer
  • Practise question by question; the ones you missed come back
  • A countdown to your next test
Class groups

Share a class with one message

Invite classmates with a link, a code, a QR code or a file. They get the lectures, transcripts, documents, class notes and a class chat with an @AI that answers from the class’s sources.

  • Compare check words to be sure who you let in
  • Organisers can require each newcomer to be let in by a member
  • Your own notes, highlights and practice stay private
Ask

Ask your class material

Ask a question and get an answer from the class’s lectures and documents, with each source one click away. Post a good answer to the class chat, or save it as a note.

  • Free models with your own OpenRouter key, or a service that you or your school run (Ollama, LM Studio, a school server)
  • You choose what AI providers may do with what you send

A closer look

Your work stays yours

Tramado has no server, no account and no telemetry.

Everything that leaves your computer, listed

Each class is an ordinary folder on your computer that you can open, copy and back up. Files travel encrypted between your class’s computers, directly or through a public Syncthing relay when a network blocks direct connections.

AI is optional and uses your own OpenRouter key, asking for free models by default. You choose what providers may do with what you send (by default, no training), and Tramado never relaxes that choice without asking you.

OpenRouter is for people aged 18 or over. If you are younger, skip AI, or connect an AI service that you or your school run.

Download Tramado

Free, for Windows, macOS and Linux. The installer includes the app and Syncthing; the speech model downloads the first time you transcribe.

This is a pre-release: keep copies of anything you cannot afford to lose, and please report problems.

The Windows and macOS installers are new and nobody has used them yet, and recording an online class (what your computer plays) has not been tested yet. If you try them, please tell us how it went: report a problem or a success.

Windows Your system

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit (x64)

Download the installer

Not code-signed yet: if SmartScreen says “Windows protected your PC”, choose More info, then Run anyway. If the computer lacks Microsoft Edge WebView2, the installer downloads it.

macOS Your system

macOS 14.2 or later, Apple silicon or Intel

Download the disk image

Drag Tramado to Applications. The first time, macOS refuses to open it: close the message, then choose System Settings › Privacy & Security › Open Anyway.

Linux Your system

x86-64; tested on Ubuntu 24.04

Download the AppImage Download the .deb

Make the AppImage executable and open it; if it does not start, install FUSE 2 (libfuse2t64 on Ubuntu 24.04). On Debian and Ubuntu you can install the .deb instead.

Local transcription needs a processor with AVX2 (most Intel and AMD processors from about 2013 on, but not some Celeron, Pentium and Atom models) or Apple silicon. Otherwise a classmate’s computer can transcribe for you, or OpenRouter (paid, about 2 cents per lecture).
