Question 2 · 4 marks

Under what conditions may the law restrict rights, freedoms and guarantees? Comment with reference to Article 18 of the Constitution.

{{sparkles}}AI draftnvidia/nemotron-3.5-super-vl:free · from 3 of your sources

Restrictions are only admissible where the Constitution expressly allows them, and must be limited to what is necessary to safeguard other constitutionally protected rights or interests 1.

Restrictive laws must be general and abstract, may not have retroactive effect, and may never reduce the essential content of the right 2. Prof. Reis stressed this last point as “the core no law can touch” 3.
